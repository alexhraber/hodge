# References and external research status

Checked 25 November 2026. Status labels describe the cited sources, not independent verification by this repository.

1. Pierre Deligne, official Hodge conjecture problem description, Clay Mathematics Institute. https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/ — general problem listed as unsolved.
2. Claire Voisin, "Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures, coniveau and algebraic cycles," Journal of Open Mathematical Problems 1 (2025), 16–51. https://doi.org/10.56994/JOMP.001.001.002 — standard coniveau framework, Gysin descriptions, support, Hodge structures.
3. Tetsuji Shioda, "The Hodge Conjecture for Fermat Varieties," Math. Ann. 245 (1979), 175–184. https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN235181684_0245/PPN235181684_0245.pdf — character criterion and inductive constructions.
4. Andreas P. Braun, Hugo Fortin, Daniel Lopez Garcia, Roberto Villaflor Loyola, "More on G-flux and general hodge cycles on the Fermat sextic," JHEP 06 (2024) 046, Theorem 1 and section 2. https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06%282024%29046 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00470 — published sextic cycle basis and arithmetic/period criteria.
5. OpenAI, "The rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties," manuscript revised 6 October 2026. https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-rational-Hodge-conjecture-for-CM-abelian-varieties-October-6-2026 — reports a proof for CM abelian varieties in all dimensions and codimensions. Formalization scope: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/032.md . We have not independently audited the paper, Lean statement or proof; no implication to general smooth projective varieties is claimed.
6. Salvatore Floccari and Lie Fu, "On the Hodge conjecture for hyper-Kähler varieties of generalized Kummer type of dimension divisible by four," 22 October 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31740 — reports the conjecture for generalized Kummer type of dimension 2n with n+1 prime, and self-powers, and a conditional extension for even n assuming the Lefschetz standard conjecture.
7. OpenAI math release history: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/history.md — records 7 October 2026 withdrawals of split abelian eightfold Weil classes, Kuga–Satake correspondences for K3 surfaces, and products of K3 surfaces after a sign error. Withdrawn results are not premises of this project.

The social-media list that motivated this work is not a reliable bibliographic record and is not adopted as evidence. No claim about resolution odds or unrelated mathematical problems is needed.
